ABOUT THIS SERVICE

Built for clarity, not control.

Dimension Threads is a developing British streetwear project. This small service supports the owner's content review workflow by retrieving limited information through TikTok's official Display API after the owner authorises access.

Limited scope

Only the permissions needed for profile and public-video metadata are requested.

Owner controlled

The owner can disconnect local access at any time. The service does not publish or alter TikTok content.

No password access

Passwords and private login details are never requested by this service.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Dimension Threads TikTok integration handles information when an owner connects a TikTok account.

1. Information accessed

With authorisation, the service may access basic TikTok profile information and metadata relating to public videos, such as video identifiers, titles or descriptions, creation time, duration and public display links, where provided by TikTok.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to support content review, analysis and planning for the Dimension Threads project. It is not used to publish content, send messages, alter an account or sell personal information.

3. Storage and security

Authorisation credentials are handled locally by the service and are not displayed to the operator. The service is designed not to request or store TikTok passwords. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so access should be disconnected if you suspect compromise.

4. Sharing and retention

Information is not intentionally sold or shared with advertisers. Local records may remain only as long as needed for the owner's review workflow and can be removed by disconnecting the integration and deleting related local data.

5. TikTok and your rights

TikTok independently processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Contact the service owner through the Dimension Threads GitHub repository for service-related questions.
